TERMS & PLATFORM RULES

Know the rules before joining.

These are the key visitor-facing rules. Read the full Terms and Privacy Policy linked below before using account features.

  • Platform status: KHELOGO is under development. Features labelled “planned” are not currently available and may change.
  • Games: Browser arcade games are for entertainment. Current games are solo mini-games; they are not online multiplayer games and scores are not cash.
  • Wallet and payouts: Displayed account data is not a promise of payment. Withdrawal requests, approvals and rewards work only when the required secure backend is deployed and operating. Never pay a fee to unlock a promised payout.
  • Creator earning: Creator profiles, monetization and future earning tools are planned possibilities, not a guarantee of income or launch date.
  • Account safety: Use accurate details, protect your password, do not create accounts to abuse referrals, and do not share OTPs or credentials.
  • Privacy: Account data is handled according to the Privacy Policy. Do not submit another person's personal information without permission.
  • Fair use: Attempts to manipulate balances, exploit bugs or misuse the platform may result in restricted access.
  • Updates: Features and rules may change as the platform develops. Check the published Terms before using new features.
